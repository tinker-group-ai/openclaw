# Market Structure Analysis Framework

Reference for systematic technical analysis methodology applied to stocks, ETFs, and crypto assets.

## Core Principles

### 1. Market Structure

- **Higher Highs & Higher Lows (HHHL)**: Uptrend confirmation
- **Lower Highs & Lower Lows (LHLL)**: Downtrend confirmation
- **Broken Market Structure (BMS)**: When price breaks below previous swing low (downtrend) or above previous swing high (uptrend)
- **Broken Market Structure Support Band (BMSB)**: The previous support level that was broken; acts as resistance on retest

### 2. Support & Resistance

- **Key Levels**: Previous swing highs/lows, psychological round numbers, historic price points
- **Support**: Price level where buying pressure historically appears; prevents downward movement
- **Resistance**: Price level where selling pressure historically appears; prevents upward movement
- **Breakout/Breakdown**: Move above resistance or below support with volume confirmation
- **Retest**: Return to a broken level; tests if it becomes new resistance/support

### 3. Moving Averages (MA)

- **200 MA**: Long-term trend indicator
- **50 MA**: Medium-term trend confirmation
- **20 MA**: Short-term support/resistance
- **Alignment**: When MAs stack (shorter above longer in uptrend), trend is strong
- **Death Cross/Golden Cross**: 50 MA crossing below/above 200 MA; major trend reversal signal

### 4. Volume & Price Action

- **Volume Confirmation**: Major moves (breakouts, breakdowns) need volume spike for confirmation
- **Declining Volume Breakout**: Lack of volume = weak breakout, likely to fail
- **Accumulation Phase**: Low volatility, tight price action, building volume (before breakout)
- **Distribution Phase**: High volatility, wider swings, declining volume (before breakdown)

### 5. Risk/Reward Analysis

- **Entry Point**: Typically at support, pullback to MA, or breakout above resistance
- **Stop Loss**: Below support level or key MA (20/50/200)
- **Target**: Next resistance level, previous swing high, or Fibonacci extension
- **Risk/Reward Ratio**: Target distance ÷ Stop loss distance; aim for 2:1 minimum

## Trend States

### Strong Uptrend

- Price above 20 MA, 20 MA above 50 MA, 50 MA above 200 MA
- Higher highs and higher lows intact
- Pullbacks to 20/50 MA are buying opportunities
- Major resistance levels define upside targets

### Established Downtrend

- Price below 20 MA, 20 MA below 50 MA, 50 MA below 200 MA
- Lower highs and lower lows intact
- Rallies to 20/50 MA are selling opportunities
- Major support levels define downside targets

### Transition/Consolidation

- Price trapped between key levels
- Volume declining (accumulation) or volatile (distribution)
- Breakout direction unclear
- Watch for volume spike + directional move

### Reversal Signals

- Death Cross (downtrend reversal) or Golden Cross (uptrend reversal)
- Break of lower lows (downtrend) and retest above BMSB
- Divergence: Price makes new high/low but momentum doesn't (RSI, MACD)

## Analysis Workflow

1. **Identify current trend state** (uptrend, downtrend, consolidation)
2. **Mark support & resistance levels** (swings, MAs, historic levels)
3. **Check MA alignment** (Are shorter MAs above/below longer MAs?)
4. **Assess recent price action** (Accumulation or distribution?)
5. **Determine key levels** (Where's the breakout/breakdown threshold?)
6. **Evaluate risk/reward** (Is risk/reward favorable?)
7. **Look for confirmation signals** (Volume, price action, divergence)
8. **Set alerts** (Support broken, resistance tested, MA crossed, volume spike)

## Common Setups

### Pullback to MA Bounce (Uptrend)

- **Setup**: Price pulls back to 20 MA during uptrend; MA still above 50/200
- **Signal**: Price bounces off MA + volume spike = continuation
- **Target**: Previous swing high or resistance level
- **Stop**: Below the MA or lower low of pullback

### Breakout Above Resistance

- **Setup**: Price consolidates at resistance; volume begins to rise
- **Signal**: Break above resistance on volume spike + close above resistance
- **Target**: Previous swing high, Fibonacci extension, or BMSB (old support)
- **Stop**: Below resistance level

### Support Breakdown + Retest

- **Setup**: Price breaks below support; BMSB becomes new resistance
- **Signal**: Bounce back and fail to take out BMSB; volume declining
- **Target**: Next support level down
- **Stop**: Above BMSB (short trade)

## Red Flags

- **Breakout with declining volume**: Weak breakout, likely to fail
- **Price far from MAs**: Overextended in either direction; vulnerable to mean reversion
- **Negative divergence**: Price makes new high but RSI/MACD doesn't (reversal signal)
- **MA Death Cross**: Major trend reversal, downside risk increases
- **Broken support**: If major support breaks on volume, downtrend likely to accelerate

## Alert Triggers

- [ ] Support level broken on volume
- [ ] Resistance level tested; volume rising
- [ ] Price crosses above/below 50 MA
- [ ] 50 MA crosses above/below 200 MA (Golden/Death Cross)
- [ ] Volume spike (2x+ average volume)
- [ ] Price touches BMSB (previous support turned resistance)
